Hsi-Wen develops a mixed-integer linear programming (MILP) model for source–sink
matching and pipeline-network design of Taiwan's carbon capture, transport &
storage (CCTS) infrastructure, integrating power-sector data with reservoir
capacity, plume-radius, and injection-well constraints.
