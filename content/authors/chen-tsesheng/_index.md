Tse-Sheng applies the Batch Adsorber Analogue Model (BAAM) to rapidly screen
adsorbents for post-combustion CO₂ capture, ranking candidate materials by
product purity, recovery and energy penalty as a fast pre-filter ahead of
detailed pressure/vacuum-swing-adsorption process optimization.
